Metal, glass and wood impacts were attenuated to leave mixing headroom. Leading
silence is skipped on playback. Material, normal closing speed and vehicle mass
control the recording, volume, pitch, tail and body resonance. A low-speed wall
bump produces panel damage without armor loss. Sustained wall friction uses the
separate scrape loop, and the burner follows the held trigger with a short fade.

The existing prerecorded dialogue remains `eleven_v4`; the reserved realtime
voice model remains `eleven_v4_turbo`. These new assets are sound effects, not
voice generation.
